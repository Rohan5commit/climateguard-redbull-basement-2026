# ClimateGuard Submission Copy

## Short Submission Blurb

ClimateGuard gives renters and homeowners an early warning system for climate risk. Enter one U.S. address and the app converts Census, FEMA, and NOAA data into a five-year risk score, top hazard, plain-language advisory, mitigation checklist, and assistance links. The production advisory path is already wired for Azure OpenAI, with Gemini and NVIDIA NIM available as demo resilience fallbacks.

## Slightly Longer Version

Millions of people only discover their climate exposure when insurance becomes harder to renew or after disaster hits. ClimateGuard changes that by turning public U.S. risk data into one household decision tool: enter an address and get a five-year score, top hazard, live alerts, practical next steps, and support programs. The platform is built on U.S. Census geocoding, FEMA National Risk Index, FEMA disaster history, and NOAA alerts, with Azure OpenAI as the production AI path and Gemini/NVIDIA NIM as fallback options for demo reliability.
